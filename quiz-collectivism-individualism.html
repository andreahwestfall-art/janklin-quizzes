Collectivism & Individualism

A self-reflection on how you balance personal identity with group belonging — and how that shapes the way you work, decide, and relate.

How this works

You will respond to 8 statements about group belonging and individual identity. Rate each from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). There are no right or wrong answers — only a clearer view of how you see the world.

What is this dimension?

From Geert Hofstede’s research, collectivism and individualism describe how people balance personal goals with group loyalty. Collectivist cultures emphasize group cohesion and shared responsibility. Individualist cultures emphasize personal autonomy and individual achievement.
